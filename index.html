About BPE

BPE Lda. is an owner-managed Portuguese investment and holding company headquartered in Montemor-o-Novo, Portugal. Founded in 2022, the company is wholly owned and led by its Managing Director, Marcus Boehm.

BPE follows an entrepreneurial investment approach: building sustainable assets through targeted capital investment, operational value creation and long-term asset management. The company invests its own capital, selectively and with a long-term horizon.

BPE is not a broker, a conventional developer or a retail financial service provider – it is a privately held investment company with its own capital, operational experience and an international outlook.

Digital Assets: Tokenisation & Bitcoin Mining

Analysis phase

BPE focuses on two areas of digital assets: the tokenisation of real assets on the blockchain and Bitcoin mining as digital production infrastructure.

ATokenisation of Real Assets

Tokenisation means representing a real asset – such as a property – or the rights to it as a digital token on a blockchain. This can make ownership structures more transparent, simplify transfers and open up new forms of financing and fractional participation. BPE analyses how its experience in real estate and financial technology can be combined in this field.

Asset Tokenisation

Digital representation of real assets and ownership rights on the blockchain.

Regulation

Assessment of legal and regulatory requirements in the EU, including crypto-asset and securities regulation.

Technology & Security

Evaluation of blockchain platforms, smart contracts, custody and security standards.

This area is in its analysis phase. BPE does not currently issue or offer tokens to the public.

BBitcoin Mining

Since 2026, BPE has been analysing and testing Bitcoin mining as an additional investment segment. To this end, BPE has acquired interests in, or economic entitlements to, mining hardware and digital mining capacity – in order to assess actual profitability and technical conditions on the basis of real production data.

Analysis focusWhat we assess
Mining RevenueBitcoin yield generated by the deployed computing power.
Operating CostsOngoing costs – in particular energy, infrastructure, operation and maintenance.
Return on InvestmentPayback period and return on capital under different market conditions.
Technology & EfficiencyComparison of mining technologies by hash rate, energy efficiency and economics.
Market RiskBitcoin price, mining difficulty, network hash rate and changing mining rewards.

This segment currently serves BPE's own investment analysis. BPE does not offer public mining investments or guaranteed-return products.

International Sourcing & Trading

In evaluation

BPE is exploring the development of an international trading activity: sourcing selected technical products and building materials directly from manufacturers in China for import into Portugal and the wider European market – using direct purchasing, optimised supply chains and efficient distribution.

Current evaluation project

Modern split air-conditioning systems, including units using the natural refrigerant R290 – assessing quality, energy efficiency, EU conformity and certification, logistics, customs and the Portuguese market.

Business model

Source · Import · Distribute

Identifying suitable, high-quality products that can be offered at competitive prices through direct international sourcing, working with local installation specialists.

This business area is currently in its economic and regulatory evaluation phase and is not yet operational. A future extension to further technical products and building materials is conceivable.
